Why this comes first
The next steps will clean up and erase things on this computer — which also erases clues about how this happened. Before you touch the computer, use your phone to grab a few screenshots so Ari can try to figure out how they got in. This takes 2 minutes and nothing you do here can make things worse.
🔑
Look through 1Password on your phone
Open the 1Password app on your phone (not this computer). Scroll through your list of saved logins. If you see anything you don't recognize — a website you never signed up for, a login for an account that isn't yours, or the same account listed twice — take a screenshot of it. Don't delete anything, just screenshot.
🗑️
Check your email's Trash / Deleted folder
Open your email app on your phone. Tap the Trash or Deleted folder. If you see emails there that you don't remember deleting — especially ones from your bank, Google, or 1Password — take a screenshot of the list.
📤
Check your email's Sent folder
Now tap your Sent folder. If there are any emails you don't remember sending, take a screenshot of those too.
📩
Text the screenshots to Ari now
Send everything you screenshotted to Ari right now, before starting Step 1 below. It doesn't matter if none of it looks important — send it anyway and let Ari sort it out.

Download and run the Cleanup Tool
Fully automatic
📥
Click the button below to download
⬇ Download Cleanup Tool
It will land in your "Downloads" folder, probably at the bottom of your screen in a small bar, or check the Downloads folder icon on your taskbar.
📂
Open the downloaded file
Find the file called CleanupTool.zip and right-click on it. Choose "Extract All..." from the menu that appears, then click the "Extract" button on the next window.
This creates a new folder called "CleanupTool" with two files inside.
🖱️
Double-click "Run-Cleanup"
Open the new CleanupTool folder and double-click the file named Run-Cleanup.

A blue box will pop up asking for permission — click "Yes".

A black window will open with white text scrolling by. This is normal — leave it alone and let it run. It will take a few minutes.

When it says "ALL DONE!", you can close the window.

Run two more scans (click-only, no typing)
Click-through
🛡️
Open Malwarebytes and remove threats
Open Malwarebytes from your desktop or Start menu. If it shows a list of items found, make sure they are all checked, then click the button labeled "Quarantine". If it asks to restart the computer, click "Yes, restart now."
🧹
Run AdwCleaner
Open your Desktop, then the CleanupTools folder. Double-click AdwCleaner. Click "Scan Now." When it finishes, click "Quarantine."
🔍
Run HitmanPro
In the same CleanupTools folder, double-click HitmanPro. Click "Next" to start scanning. When it's done, click "Next" again to remove anything it finds.

Get Chrome back with your bookmarks
Fully automatic
🌐
Install Chrome again
Open the blue Edge browser (it's already on your computer). Type google.com/chrome in the address bar at the top and press Enter. Click the blue "Download Chrome" button, then open the downloaded file and click through the installer.
⭐
Bring your bookmarks back
Open the new Chrome. Click the three dots in the top-right corner → Bookmarks → Import Bookmarks and Settings. Choose "Bookmarks HTML file" and select the file on your Desktop inside the CleanupTools folder called bookmarks_backup.
